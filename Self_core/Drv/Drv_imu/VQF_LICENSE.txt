VQF six-axis C port

Adapted from https://github.com/dlaidig/vqf
Upstream revision: 86ba56bdd3158b9b05f9f9fe5596866ba326438c
Source: vqf/cpp/vqf.cpp and vqf/cpp/vqf.hpp

This port retains the six-axis online algorithm, including motion/rest bias
estimation. Magnetometer processing and offline estimation are omitted.
Quaternion and bias arithmetic uses float; Butterworth coefficients and history
use double as in the upstream single-precision implementation. The driver uses
g and converts to m/s^2 internally; coefficient changes also support variable dt.
